## 5. Current Technology Interests

### 6G and AI-Native Networks

I am interested in what 6G can enable beyond faster connectivity.

The emergence of AI-native devices and applications may change how people interact with networks and what they expect from connectivity.

I believe the industry should focus on identifying real application requirements rather than developing network capabilities without clear use cases.

### Integrated Sensing and Communication (ISAC)

ISAC combines wireless communication and radio-based sensing capabilities.

Potential applications include drone detection, object monitoring, and improving situational awareness.

I am interested in understanding the practical feasibility of ISAC, including:

- Detection accuracy and range
- Spectrum bandwidth requirements
- Line-of-sight limitations
- Environmental conditions
- Integration with existing network infrastructure
- Commercial applications and business value

My focus is on moving beyond demonstrations to understanding whether sensing can become a reliable and useful network service.

### Network Autonomy

AI has the potential to change how networks are managed and operated.

I am interested in the evolution toward autonomous networks, particularly the use of AI for:

- Network optimization
- Automated operations
- Closed-loop control
- Fault detection and resolution
- Network resilience

The objective is not simply greater automation, but measurable improvements in operational efficiency, service quality, and reliability.

### AI Wearables and Connected Experiences

I believe AI-powered wearables, particularly smart glasses, could introduce new ways for people to interact with digital services.

Potential applications include contextual assistance, real-time information, and hands-free experiences.

I am interested in the relationship between devices, AI models, networks, edge computing, and cloud services.

User experience is critical. Factors such as latency, reliability, convenience, and ease of interaction often matter more than technical specifications alone.

### Personal and Family AI Experiences

I am also interested in AI assistants that can understand user-authorized personal and family context.

Today's AI services are often designed around individual interactions. There may be opportunities to create more useful experiences that support everyday activities across households.

Privacy, security, trust, and user control should be fundamental to these services.

## 6. My Philosophy on Innovation

### Start with the Real Problem

Technology should serve a purpose.

Before developing a new capability, I believe we should understand what problem it solves and who benefits from it.

### Prototype More. Measure More. Assume Less.

I believe in building working prototypes early and testing assumptions through actual measurements.

Real-world performance often reveals issues that are difficult to anticipate from theoretical analysis.

### Technology Is Not the End Goal

A technically impressive solution is not necessarily a successful product.

A useful innovation should create measurable value for customers, businesses, or society.

### Focus on the End-to-End Experience

Technology solutions should be evaluated from the user's perspective.

For example, improving network performance may have limited impact if most of the delay comes from applications, devices, or AI processing.

Understanding the entire system is essential.

### Combine Technical and Business Perspectives

My experience across technology vendors and network operators has taught me that technical feasibility and commercial viability must be considered together.

Successful innovation requires both.
